## What the run does not establish

- **Anything section 5 of the Spec leaves out.** A deliberate party racing the run, or a
  change made and undone between the window's two reads, is outside what the harness
  claims, and the per-load hash that would cover it is not bought.
- **The baseline's own limits carry over.** karl declares no loop file, the model is the
  0.5b, and time to first token is not measured. No clock was held, and its state is
  recorded instead.
- **Agreement with the Ampere and Ada cells.** The run serves this box's own engine
  library build, `libggml-cuda` `abec199d`, not olympus's. Whether the four cells agree
  on one set of library bytes is #698's library-matched rerun, not this run.
- **The cause of run4's 13:24 fault.** Its absence here over 13,710 loads says the
  doubled line is rare, not why it happened once.
- **The settings on any other box or workload.** They held for one agent on this box
  over seven hours. A second agent, or a longer run, doubles or extends the volume, and
  2G of 4G is the margin this run left. Nothing here speaks for olympus.
