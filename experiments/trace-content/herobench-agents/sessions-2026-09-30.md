# HeroBench sessions, 2026-09-30

A dated run, read by no gate, taken to move the classifier evaluation plan's session
count toward the 50 it requires before any fitting,
`experiments/trace-content/classifier-evaluation/PLAN.md` sections 1 and 4, the
split's unit being the session. It was not taken to win tasks. The sampler is still
frozen, per #746, so the call format's decay was expected to recur, and it did.

The deposit is `herobench-sessions-2026-09-30` on the shared bulk store. It holds each
session's `run.json` and task file, the agents' traces and copied state stores, the box
facts, the declarations each session was a copy of, and `SHA256SUMS`, and none of it is
copied here. Every figure below is in `rusty/report.json` there, which
`code/sessions_report.py` wrote, and `COMMANDS.md` gives the commands.

**The decay.** A turn is decayed when its verbatim emission opened a call and never
closed it, the signature #746 records. 30 of the 50 sessions decayed, 95 of their 392
turns. The tee reading, #758, reads where in a run it sets in.

**The positions.** 9,268 over 392 turns, a median of 3 a turn and a maximum of 238. A
position is what the plan's section 2 names, counted by `code/sessions_report.py`, which
reproduces the counted pair's 978 positions, median 4 and maximum 132 over session
`s-rusty-b`. **The plan's section 8 gate 3 recomputes the per-turn maximum over the
fitting corpus, and over these 50 sessions it reads 238**, where the preliminary pair
read 132. The gate's text is unchanged, being a rule that recomputes rather than a
figure.

**The typed landing.** The store typed 4,018 messages, their parts 2,405 text, 1,627
tool calls and 1,613 tool results, and 1,661 measurements with 305 series readings.
4,207 fields landed verbatim, every one a measurement reading, because the installed
stack was deployed from `99494511` before #748's `float_roundtrip`, per the tee
reading's section 5, #758.

**The weights hash.** Sessions `s-rusty-n-001` to `006` carry `weights_hash` as the
